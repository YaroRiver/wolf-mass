Log a 4 × 4

Average watts for each interval. One target for all four, spread within 3%.

How it progresses

  • Spread within 3% and RPE 8 or lower: next week add 5–10 W, or a fifth interval.
  • Spread over 3%: keep the same watts.
  • W7 to W11: odd weeks 5 × 4 min, even weeks 3 × 13 × 30 s on, 15 s off.
  • Apple Cardio Fitness only updates from outdoor walks, runs and hikes, so watts are the measure in this block.

Targets

Creatine monohydrate 5 g every day. At least 170 g protein, 35–45 g a meal.

Adjusting from W3

  • Under 0.15 kg a week for two weeks: add 150 kcal of carbs.
  • 0.2 to 0.35 kg a week: hold.
  • Over 0.4 kg a week, or waist up 1 cm in two weeks: take off 150 kcal.
  • W1–W2: if weight holds within 0.3 kg after the creatine water, 3450 is maintenance.

Knee

  • Pain rising during BBJ, sleds, lunges or any leg exercise, or the movement changes: that exercise is done for the day.
  • Pain, swelling or a limp the next day: review the week's leg load.
  • Sports physio before any running progression.
  • Running back, after physio: 6 × 200 m, then 400, 600 and 1000 m, with pain at 2/10 or lower during and back to baseline next morning.

Illness

  • Fever or symptoms below the neck: full rest until 24 hours without fever; first session back at 3 RIR with half the sets.
  • Nose and throat only, no fever: Engine and Saturday off, lifting at 3 RIR.
  • Calories and protein stay on plan.

Lifting

  • Legs never to failure. Main lifts 1–2 RIR. Upper-body isolation, last set 0–1 RIR.
  • All sets at the top of the range: next time +2.5 kg, dumbbells +2 kg, isolation one step.
  • The session ends on the written volume.
  • A missed session is skipped. Friday catches a missed Upper B.
  • Elbow pain: one biceps set less a week, preacher curl becomes hammer curl.
  • Back sore the day after sumo squats: the exercise goes.

Recovery

  • Bed at 21:30, give or take 30 minutes, every night. 8–9 hours in bed.
  • Caffeine before 13:00. Engine starts before 18:00.
  • Two of these, sleep under 6.5 h, resting HR up 5, HRV low 3 days: cut that day's sets by 30% and keep the loads.
