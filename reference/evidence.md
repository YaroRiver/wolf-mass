# Evidence behind v3

| Decision | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Concurrent training keeps whole-muscle hypertrophy | Schumann et al. 2022, Sports Med — https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s40279-021-01587-7 |
| Running interferes more with fibre hypertrophy than cycling → VO2 on BikeErg | Lundberg et al. 2022, Sports Med — https://link.springer.com/doi/10.1007/s40279-022-01688-x |
| ~10 sets per muscle per week, each muscle ≥ 2×/week; athletes need sport-specific plans | ACSM 2026 position stand — https://acsm.org/resistance-training-guidelines-update-2026/ |
| Volume dose-response with diminishing returns; per-session plateau ~11 fractional sets | Pelland et al.; Remmert et al. 2025 — https://sportrxiv.org/index.php/server/preprint/view/460 |
| Closer to failure matters for hypertrophy; 1–2 RIR ≈ failure | Robinson et al. 2024; Refalo et al. 2024 — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38393985/ |
| Overhead triceps extension: 1.4–1.5× growth vs pushdown | Maeo et al. 2023 — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35819335/ |
| Incline curl → proximal biceps, preacher → distal | Nunes et al. 2025 — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39809454/ |
| DB and cable lateral raises equal for side-delt growth | Larsen et al. 2025 — https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/physiology/articles/10.3389/fphys.2025.1611468/full |
| Stretch emphasis / full ROM; lengthened partials ≈ full ROM in trained | Wolf et al. 2025, PeerJ — https://peerj.com/articles/18904 |
| Regional hypertrophy by muscle length is weak | Varovic et al. 2024 — https://sportrxiv.org/index.php/server/preprint/download/464/988/924 |
| Incline bench biases clavicular pec (EMG) | https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7449336 |
| Protein ~1.6 g/kg plateau; masters need larger per-meal doses | Morton 2018 — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28698222/ ; GSSI masters review |
| Larger surplus mostly adds fat in trained lifters | Helms et al. 2023 — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37914977/ |
| Creatine + resistance training adds lean mass in older adults | 2025 meta-analysis — https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC12752335/ |
| Apple Cardio Fitness counts only outdoor walk/run/hike | Apple Support — https://support.apple.com/en-gb/HT211856 |
