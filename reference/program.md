# Program v3 — 12-Week Mass + Hybrid Block

Editable master copy: Claude Doc "12-тижнева програма: +5 кг маси з гібридним HYROX" (v3). This file mirrors the training part for Claude Code.

## Week

| Day | Session | Sets | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mon | Lower | 15 | 65–70 min |
| Tue | Upper A — strength + hypertrophy | 23 | 75 min |
| Wed | Engine — BikeErg VO2 + arm/delt/abs block | 11 | 50–60 min |
| Thu | Upper B — upper chest first | 21 | 75 min |
| Fri | Rest · spare day for a missed Upper B | — | — |
| Sat | HYROX Hybrid · A odd weeks / B even weeks | — | 35–45 min work |
| Sun | Recovery walk 30–60 min | — | — |

Weekly direct sets from W3: back 13 · chest 13 (6 upper, 6 mid, 1 dips) · quads 13 · side delts 10 · biceps 9 · triceps 6 + presses · abs 3 · rear delts 2 + rows · hamstrings 2.

## Sessions

### Lower (Mon)
1. Leg extension 2 × 12–15 · 2 RIR · warm-up role
2. Seated leg curl 2 × 8–12 · 1–2 RIR
3. Leg press 2 × 8–12 · 2 RIR · ~160 kg
4. Bulgarian split squat 3 × 8–10 / leg · 2 RIR · 24 + 24 kg
5. Heavy walking lunges 4 × 20 m · 2 RIR · 32 + 32 kg
6. Sumo squat, heavy DB 2 × 8–12 · 2 RIR · back sore next day → remove

### Upper A (Tue)
1. Bench press 4 × 5–8 · 1–2 RIR · 80 kg (W9+: 3 × 6–10 + back-off −10%)
2. Lateral raise, standing DB, 4 × 12–20 · 0–1 RIR · between bench sets
3. Weighted pull-up 4 × 5–8 · 1–2 RIR · +20 kg (+1 set from W4)
4. Chest-supported row 3 × 8–12 · 1–2 RIR (+1 set W8 if recovering)
5. Low-to-high cable fly 2 × 12–15 · 1 RIR
6. EZ preacher curl 3 × 8–12 + overhead cable triceps extension 3 × 8–12 (superset) · 1 RIR (extension +1 set W7)

### Engine (Wed)
- W1: arm/delt block only, optional. W2: one BikeErg 4 × 4 test for baseline watts.
- W3–W5: 4 × 4 min BikeErg at RPE 8, 3 min easy, one watt target, spread ≤ 3%.
- W7–W11: odd weeks 5 × 4 min · even weeks 3 × 13 × 30/15 s.
- Gate from W3: VO2 drops when 2 of 3 hit — sleep < 6.5 h · RHR +5 vs 14-day · legs heavy after Monday.
- Block: hammer curl 3 × 10–15 · side-lying lateral raise 3 × 12–20 / arm · cable pushdown 2 × 10–15 · cable crunch 3 × 10–15.

### Upper B (Thu)
1. Incline DB press 30° 4 × 8–12 · 1–2 RIR · first
2. Lat pulldown neutral 3 × 8–12 · 1 RIR (+1 set from W4)
3. One-arm DB row 3 × 8–12 / arm · 1–2 RIR · 35 → 45 kg (+1 set W8 if recovering)
4. Cable fly / pec deck 2 × 10–15 · 1 RIR
5. Weighted dips 1 × 6–10 · 1–2 RIR · +20 kg
6. Side-lying lateral raise 3 × 12–20 / arm + reverse pec deck 2 × 12–20 (superset) · 0–1 RIR
7. Incline DB curl 3 × 10–15 · 1 RIR (+1 set W7)

### HYROX A — Heavy Sleds (odd weeks)
1. Sled push 4 × 20 m @ 220 kg (higher by feel) · stop sleds if a set is > 5 s slower than set 1
2. Sled pull 3 × 20 m @ 153–180 kg
3. 2 rounds: 1000 m erg + 25 Wall Balls @ 9 kg · 90 s rest

### HYROX B — Race Flow (even weeks)
4 rounds: 750 m erg + station — sled push 2 × 12.5 m @ 202 kg / BBJ 40 m / farmers 200 m @ 2 × 32 kg / 40 Wall Balls. Round-4 erg within ±3 s of round 1.
BBJ: knee sensation on landing → BBJ over, swap 40 WB. Knee worse next morning → BBJ off, back after 2 clean weeks from 20 m.

## Week modifiers

- W1–W2: isolation −1 set, everything 3 RIR (biceps 2–3).
- W6 and W12: deload — sets −50%, loads kept, 3–4 RIR, checks at 1 RIR only, Saturday light B without BBJ.
- W5: Christmas Fri 25 Dec. W10: Australia Day Mon 26 Jan → Lower Tue, Upper A Wed, Engine Fri.
